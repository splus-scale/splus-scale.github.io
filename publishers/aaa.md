The Argentine Astronomical Association (Asociación Argentina de Astronomía, AAA) is a non-profit civil entity founded in 1958 to advance astronomy, astrophysics, and related sciences in Argentina. The association supports scientific research by organizing annual meetings, aids early-career researchers through grants and fellowships, and publishes periodicals such as the _Boletín de la Asociación Argentina de Astronomía_ to disseminate scientific work from the regional astronomical community.
