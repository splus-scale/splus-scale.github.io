_The Astronomical Journal_ (AJ) is a leading peer-reviewed journal dedicated to original astronomical research, with an emphasis on significant scientific results derived from observations. Founded in 1849 by Benjamin Apthorp Gould and owned by the American Astronomical Society (AAS), AJ covers astronomical surveys, instrumentation, data analysis methods, software and computational tools, and observational discoveries across all wavelengths.
