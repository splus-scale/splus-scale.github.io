_The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series_ (ApJS) specializes in extensive research papers, foundational compilations, and large-scale astronomical datasets that support the international astrophysics community. Published on behalf of the American Astronomical Society, ApJS features extensive source catalogs, major survey data releases, complex numerical simulations, and technical descriptions of state-of-the-art observatories and instruments.
