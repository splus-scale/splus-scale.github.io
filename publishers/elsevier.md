Elsevier is a global leader in information and analytics, helping researchers and healthcare professionals advance science and improve health outcomes. Part of the RELX Group, Elsevier publishes over 2,800 peer-reviewed journals, operates platforms including ScienceDirect and Scopus, and delivers cutting-edge digital solutions for scientific research, data analysis, and clinical reference.
