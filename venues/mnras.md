_Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society_ (MNRAS) is one of the world’s leading primary research journals in astronomy and astrophysics. Published by Oxford University Press on behalf of the Royal Astronomical Society since 1827, MNRAS publishes peer-reviewed research papers and fast-track letters covering observational, theoretical, and instrumental investigations spanning stars, galaxies, cosmology, and planetary systems.
