_Astronomy and Computing_ is a peer-reviewed scientific journal dedicated to the intersection of astronomy, computer science, and data engineering. Published by Elsevier, the journal serves as a dedicated platform for computational astrophysics, focusing on scientific software engineering, big-data management pipelines, high-performance computing, interactive data visualization, and open-source data stewardship in modern observational and theoretical astronomy.
