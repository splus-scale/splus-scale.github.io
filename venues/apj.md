_The Astrophysical Journal_ (ApJ) is a flagship peer-reviewed scientific journal devoted to major developments, discoveries, and theories in astronomy and astrophysics. Founded in 1895 by George Ellery Hale and James Edward Keeler, ApJ is owned by the American Astronomical Society and published by IOP Publishing, covering comprehensive theoretical, observational, and computational contributions.
