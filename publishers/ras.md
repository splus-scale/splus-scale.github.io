Founded in 1820, the Royal Astronomical Society (RAS) encourages and promotes the study of astronomy, solar-system science, geophysics, and closely related branches of science. Headquartered at Burlington House in London, the Society represents researchers across the UK and internationally, publishes prestigious academic journals such as _Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society_ (MNRAS) and _Geophysical Journal International_ (GJI), convenes scientific meetings, and supports public education and scientific heritage.
