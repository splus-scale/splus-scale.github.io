_Notas Técnicas do CBPF_ is a peer-reviewed scientific journal edited by the Brazilian Center for Research in Physics (CBPF/MCTI). Published since 1961, the journal presents methodological investigations, technical reports, scientific instrumentation, and computational solutions developed to support physics and multidisciplinary applications.
