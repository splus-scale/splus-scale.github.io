_The Astrophysical Journal Letters_ (ApJL) is a rapid-publication journal dedicated to high-impact, time-sensitive discoveries across all fields of astronomy and astrophysics. Published by IOP Publishing on behalf of the American Astronomical Society, ApJL provides concise, high-priority research communications on urgent observational results and transformative theoretical developments.
