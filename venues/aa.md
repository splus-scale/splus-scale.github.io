_Astronomy & Astrophysics_ (A&A) is an international peer-reviewed journal publishing original research in theoretical, observational, and instrumental astronomy and astrophysics. Established in 1969 through the merger of several national European astronomical journals, A&A is sponsored by scientific organisations across European and associated countries in partnership with the European Southern Observatory (ESO), published by EDP Sciences under a Subscribe-to-Open (S2O) model.
