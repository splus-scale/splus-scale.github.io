The _Boletín de la Asociación Argentina de Astronomía_ (BAAA) is the official peer-reviewed scientific publication of the Argentine Astronomical Association. It documents research papers, oral presentations, and posters presented at the Association’s annual meetings, serving as an archive of astronomical and astrophysical research in the region.
