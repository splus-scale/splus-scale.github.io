The Brazilian Center for Research in Physics (_Centro Brasileiro de Pesquisas Físicas_, CBPF) is a scientific research institute affiliated with the Brazilian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI). Founded in 1949, CBPF conducts basic and applied research in physics, coordinates graduate education programs, and develops scientific instrumentation and technological innovation.
