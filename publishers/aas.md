The American Astronomical Society (AAS) is the major organization of professional astronomers in North America, dedicated to enhancing and sharing humanity’s scientific understanding of the universe as a diverse and inclusive astronomical community. Established in 1899, the AAS publishes world-leading research journals—including _The Astronomical Journal_ and _The Astrophysical Journal_ — hosts premier scientific conferences, and advocates for astronomy and science policy worldwide.
