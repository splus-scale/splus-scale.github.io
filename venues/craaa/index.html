Asociación Argentina de Astronomía

The Cuaderno de Resúmenes de la Asociación Argentina de Astronomía (CRAAA) compiles the abstracts, posters, and presentation summaries from the annual scientific meetings of the Argentine Astronomical Association. Published by the Asociación Argentina de Astronomía, it documents current research activities, preliminary findings, and ongoing astronomical surveys across the regional and international scientific community.
