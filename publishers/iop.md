IOP Publishing is a society-owned scientific publisher through which scientific research is distributed worldwide. Wholly owned by the Institute of Physics (IOP), any surplus generated is reinvested back into the scientific community to promote research, education, and public engagement in physics. IOP Publishing partners with scientific societies—including the American Astronomical Society—to deliver peer-reviewed journals, conference proceedings, books, and science news platforms such as _Physics World_.
